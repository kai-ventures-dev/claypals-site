How it works

Two easy puzzles, then the word.

Every food works the same way, in three steps. Nothing is timed. Your child can take as long as they need.

  1. 1

    Slide it home

    Put the apple in the orchard, the carrot in the garden, the grapes on the vine. The target is big, pieces snap in easily, and nothing needs turning. There are no wrong answers.

  2. 2

    Build it back

    The food breaks into three or four big clay pieces, sized for a two-year-old's hands. Put them back and it is whole again. A piece in the wrong place just pops back out.

  3. Apple
    3

    Then it says the word

    The finished food says its name out loud. Then each letter is named on a little word card. It takes a few seconds and is never a quiz. A grown-up can turn the letters off in the Parents' Corner (the grown-ups' settings in the app).

Questions parents ask

Questions, answered.

What is ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies?

ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies is a puzzle app for iPhone and iPad, made for children aged 2 to 4. It has 28 clay fruits and vegetables that say their own names. There are no ads, no subscription and no data collected. Seven foods are free. The other 21 cost $4.99, one time. It is made by Kai Ventures.

How much does ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies cost? Is it a subscription?

ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies is free to download, and the apple, banana, carrot, tomato, orange, corn and grape are free to keep. One optional in-app purchase, All Foods, unlocks the other 21 foods for $4.99, one time (£4.99 in the UK). It is not a subscription. There are no coins, no bundles and no ads.

Why pay once for ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies instead of using a free app with ads?

With ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies, you can try seven foods for free and unlock the rest for $4.99, one time. So there are no ads for your child to tap and no subscription to cancel. Many free kids' apps make money from ads, and many learning apps charge every month. Check an app's privacy label before you choose.

Does ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies collect data or show ads?

No. ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies shows no ads and collects no data. Its Apple privacy label says Data Not Collected. The app has no analytics (usage tracking), accounts or microphone, and progress is saved only on the device. The claypals.app website is separate. It uses Google Analytics, PostHog and the Meta Pixel (tools that count visits and measure ads).

Does ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies follow children's privacy laws like COPPA?

ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies collects no personal information from anyone. So it collects none from children under COPPA (the US law on children's privacy online) or the GDPR (the privacy law in the EU and UK). Its Apple privacy label says Data Not Collected, and deleting the app removes everything it saved. Full policy: claypals.app/produce/privacy.html.

Does ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies work offline?

Yes. ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies works fully offline, including in airplane mode. You can use it on a plane, in a waiting room or anywhere without Wi-Fi. The app does not connect to the internet on its own. It only uses Apple's App Store if a grown-up chooses to buy the full set of foods.

Which devices does ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies work on?

ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies works on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. It is not available for Android, and Apple lists it as not verified for Mac. The app is in English only. It does not need Wi-Fi, an account or any other app to play.

What age is ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies for?

ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies is made for children aged 2 to 4. The pieces are big enough for a two-year-old's hands. Nothing is timed, and there are no wrong answers. On the App Store it has a 4+ age rating (suitable for all ages) and is in the Kids category for ages 5 and under.

How do I stop my toddler buying things in ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies?

In ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies, the only purchase is behind a parental gate: a grown-up has to hold a button for three seconds. Apple's Ask to Buy (a parent approves each purchase) and Family Sharing work as normal. There is only one thing to buy, so the most anyone can spend is $4.99.

Can I turn off the letters or the sound in ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies?

Yes. In ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies, a grown-up can turn the letters off in the Parents' Corner (the grown-ups' settings in the app). The background music is soft and turns off with one tap. The app goes silent when the mute switch is on. It plays quietly under a podcast or music and does not stop it.

What is the difference between ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies and ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles?

ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies and ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles are separate ClayPals apps. Fruits & Veggies has clay fruits and vegetables, and Toddler Puzzles has clay animals. Both are for ages 2 to 4 and play the same way. Each has its own free items and its own one-time unlock, so buying one does not unlock the other.

Is ClayPals a clay-modeling app?

No. ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies is a puzzle app with clay animation art. A child slides a clay fruit or vegetable into its scene, builds it back from big pieces and hears its name. It does not teach clay modeling or sculpting, and you do not need real clay or anything else to play.
