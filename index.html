How it works

Two easy puzzles, then the word.

Every animal works the same way, in three steps. Nothing is timed. Your child can take as long as they need.

  1. 1

    Slide the animal home

    Put the puppy in the sunny backyard, the penguin on the ice, the octopus in the reef. The target is big, pieces snap in easily, and nothing needs turning. There are no wrong answers.

  2. 2

    Build it back

    The animal breaks into three or four big clay pieces, sized for a two-year-old's hands. Put them back and it is whole again. A piece in the wrong place just pops back out.

  3. DOG
    3

    Then it says the word

    The finished animal says its name out loud. Each letter is named on a little clay card, then the whole word is said again. It takes a few seconds and is never a quiz. A parent can turn it off with one tap.

Questions parents ask

Questions, answered.

What is ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles?

ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles is a puzzle app for iPhone and iPad, made for children aged 2 to 4. It has 23 clay animals that say their own names. There are no ads or subscriptions. Three animals are free. An optional one-time purchase unlocks the other 20. It is made by Kai Ventures.

How much does ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles cost? Is it a subscription?

ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles is free to download, and the dog, duck and cow are free to keep. One optional in-app purchase, All Animals, unlocks the other 20 animals. The regular U.S. price is $4.99. Prices and offers vary by storefront; check the current price in the app. It is not a subscription. There are no coins, no bundles and no ads.

Why pay once for ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles instead of using a free app with ads?

With ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles, you can try three animals for free and unlock the rest with one optional in-app purchase. So there are no ads for your child to tap and no subscription to cancel. Many free kids' apps make money from ads, and many learning apps charge every month. Check an app's privacy label before you choose.

What does the U.S. App Store list for ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles privacy?

The U.S. App Store privacy label for ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles says “Data Not Collected.” This is about the app, not the separate ClayPals website. Learn more: claypals.app/privacy.html.

Where can I read the ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles privacy policy?

The privacy policy explains how ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles handles information and how the separate website works. Full policy: claypals.app/privacy.html.

Does ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles work offline?

Yes. ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles works fully offline, including in airplane mode. You can use it on a plane, in a waiting room or anywhere without Wi-Fi. The optional unlock uses the App Store.

Which devices does ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles work on?

ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles works on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. It is not available for Android, and Apple lists it as not verified for Mac. The app is in English only. The puzzles can be played offline.

What age is ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles for?

ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles is made for children aged 2 to 4. The pieces are big enough for a two-year-old's hands. Nothing is timed, and there are no wrong answers. On the App Store it has a 4+ age rating (suitable for all ages) and is in the Kids category for ages 5 and under.

How do I stop my toddler buying things in ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles?

In ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles, the only purchase is behind a parental gate: a grown-up has to hold a button for three seconds. There is only one optional unlock. Check the app for the price in your storefront.

Can I turn off the words in ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles?

Yes. The U.S. App Store description for ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles says the word feature can be turned off.

What is the difference between ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles and ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies?

ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles and ClayPals: Fruits & Veggies are separate ClayPals apps. Toddler Puzzles has clay animals, and Fruits & Veggies has clay fruits and vegetables. Both are for ages 2 to 4 and play the same way. Each has its own free items and its own one-time unlock, so buying one does not unlock the other.

Is ClayPals a clay-modeling app?

No. ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles is a puzzle app with clay animation art. A child slides a clay animal into its scene, builds it back from big pieces and hears its name. It does not teach clay modeling or sculpting, and you do not need real clay or anything else to play.
