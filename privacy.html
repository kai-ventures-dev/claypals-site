ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

The short version: ClayPals collects no data at all. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no third-party services. Everything your child does in the app stays on your device.

Who we are

ClayPals: Toddler Puzzles is a claymation puzzle app for children ages 2–4, made by Kai Ventures. Because our players are very young children, we designed the app to the strictest possible privacy standard: it simply does not collect, store, or share any personal information — from children or from parents.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, or share:

  • No names, email addresses, or account information (there are no accounts)
  • No photos, camera, microphone, contacts, or location access
  • No device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • No usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioral data
  • No data from or about children — or anyone else

The only information the app saves is puzzle progress and whether the one-time unlock has been purchased. That information is stored only on your device (and in your device's normal backups) and never leaves it.

No ads, no tracking

ClayPals contains no advertising of any kind and performs no tracking. We do not use third-party analytics, ad networks, social media integrations, or any other third-party services. The app has no external links a child can reach.

The app also makes no internet connections of its own: every puzzle, picture, sound, and animation is built into the app, and it works fully in airplane mode. The only network activity related to ClayPals is Apple's own App Store system handling the optional one-time purchase, described below.

Purchases

ClayPals offers a single one-time purchase that unlocks all animals — no subscriptions, no consumables. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see or store any payment information. The purchase screen sits behind a parental gate that requires an adult-level motor task, and Apple's Ask to Buy and Family Sharing features are fully supported. Apple's handling of your payment data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

ClayPals is offered in the Kids category of the App Store and is built for children under 5. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children as defined by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR, or similar laws anywhere in the world. There is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete — deleting the app removes everything it ever saved.

Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before that update ships. Given the app's design, we do not expect that to happen.
